## Generation prescriptions

| Band/profile | Generate or retain | Omit only where supported | Existing-work relationship |
|---|---|---|---|
| A — readable wall | Readable planes, corners, shelves, convex changes and road-ground contact; appropriate fine material appearance | Omit geometric pores/grain/tiny cracks where shape stays intact; provide their appearance in the material; no uniform micro-bevel pass | Use accepted Component 230 v8 as the close-rock comparison fixture; apply a future bounded recipe only to eligible mapped rock |

| B — dominant broad face | Characteristic outline, major planes, fractures, shelves and forms responsible for large shading | Fine rounding, repeated chips and excessive material layers unsupported by views | Derive and prove a broader-form treatment before rollout; existing v8 does not automatically authorize a new B recipe |
| C — separate panorama | Source landform, skyline, large divisions and broad coherent material variation/scale | Unsupported fine interior geometry, after checking closer appearances | Retain the existing macro Landscape; add only justified presentation geometry; no blanket mesh replacement |
| D — confirmed hidden | Needed shadow/reflection/support/continuity contribution | Cosmetic finishing after reviewed camera/access coverage establishes invisibility | No D assigned in this survey; do not create it from lack of a sampled view |
| U — unresolved | Existing source geometry and coherent material baseline; investigate coverage/correspondence | No inferred downgrade or automatic geometry operation | Explicit unresolved input, distinct from D |

A near-wall observation and a B observation can refer to the same physical face. Once identity is established, retain each supported detail scale across views. A C view cannot downgrade a surface with readable A/B forms elsewhere. A B massif's own outline remains protected under B; the outline alone does not establish a separate low-detail C region. No numeric distance rings, triangle targets, LOD settings, visibility durations or costs are inferred from stills.

## End-to-end sequence

1. **Keep the source scene:** retain native macro terrain, road, earthworks and accepted v8 reference with their identities.
2. **Screen the captures:** this prestudy provides 669 paired observations, near-side notes, B/C evidence and uncertainty. It selects work and representative checks.
3. **Resolve physical surfaces:** register the actual owning mesh/Landscape/source patches and boundaries against original PNGs and additional views. A camera look target is not a rock position. Surface footprints and stable IDs are still missing here.
4. **Prepare separate masks:** material semantics continue from existing rock/soil/vegetation evidence; detail-demand masks reference reviewed physical surfaces. Preserve unmapped coverage rather than painting an arbitrary radius around a camera.
5. **Select treatment:** use the five independent tags plus required detail scales. GEO_FIX requires a specific observed defect; SILHOUETTE_CRITICAL protects the outline; HERO_DETAIL focuses quality; BACKGROUND_LOW_PRIORITY remains a reviewed simplification candidate; MATERIAL_TEST_CANDIDATE selects controlled comparisons. Tags do not execute edits.
6. **Process owned meshes:** reuse existing native export/GeometryProcessing foundations. For A rock, compare against v8; for B retain large forms; for C avoid unnecessary fine processing. Source, eligibility, interfaces and ownership remain fixed. Native Landscape cannot represent every overhang or vertical rock feature; owned presentation meshes supply justified forms.
7. **Assemble and shade:** a later version-verified PCG/PCGEx consumer uses the mapped requirements and admitted recipes. Materials provide fine appearance and semantic transitions. Avoid overlapping visible ground surfaces; any replacement/hiding must follow its separately reviewed ownership contract.
8. **Add later dressing where justified:** scree, stones and vegetation consume existing semantic masks and exclusions under their work-item dependencies. This prestudy does not start those implementations or scatter objects uniformly by band.
9. **Review fixed views:** compare reference/candidate with the same cameras, geometry/material control and lighting as applicable, both directions and additional approaches. Check shapes, texture scale, contact, silhouettes and detail transitions.
10. **Persist and propagate only after proof:** record source/config identities, bounded affected scope, restoration/regeneration and applicable owner/technical admission. Current lighting/PCGEx failures and later performance requirements remain unchanged; this task measures no performance.

## Exactly what v8 contributes

The accepted `rounded-limestone-reshape-v8` profile (`native-source-rock-reshape`, `limestone_local_reshape`) operates on the owned native Component 230 export using source-exact selective refinement and bounded normal-space relaxation. Its local fixture permits at most 50 cm total 3D movement from the original native surface, fixes protected interfaces/normals and retains the original footprint and material with 3 m physical UV scale. Existing checks include the 60,000-triangle ceiling and at least 10% original XY triangle area.

The owner accepted the local appearance at `4f2cba560d54931dc8ba080370d96a7aad24f15b`. These are that fixture's limits and evidence, not map-wide geometry budgets or authorization to apply the same recipe to every Landscape component. The combined lighting and separate PCGEx admission remain failing as recorded in the [cliff contract](../../tooling/SA_CALOBRA_CLIFF_EROSION_PASS.md). Existing work is reused; no heights, accepted geometry, materials, camera capture or settings change here.
